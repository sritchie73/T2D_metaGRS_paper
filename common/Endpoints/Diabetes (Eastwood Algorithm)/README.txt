Adjudicated diabetes status (Eastwood et al. 2016)
===================================================

Files in this folder relate to the adjudication of prevalent and incident 
diabetes status and type based on self-reported medical history, medication
usage, and linked hospital records using the algorithms described in Eastwood
et al. 2016 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27631769/. See Figures 2 and 3
for flowcharts showing the adjudication of prevalent and incident diabetes
respectively.

As with other datasets in common/ row correspond to unique participant-visit
combinations, where the visit_index indicates the UK Biobank assessment relative
to which the incident and prevalent status and follow-up has been adjudicated 
for.
